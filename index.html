Profile

About

I'm a quantitative researcher with a background in mathematical sciences, spatial epidemiology, and geospatial data science. My work involves multi-country datasets, environmental information, and spatial analysis, using R and Python to develop statistical and spatial models.

I am interested in how environmental and climate-related factors intersect with infectious disease risk, urban growth, and changing landscapes. I aim to build reproducible analytical workflows and produce spatial evidence that can contribute to public health research and climate adaptation.
